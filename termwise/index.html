Your Google account and data

Google sign-in lets you choose the account to connect. Termwise retrieves account metadata and Google Ads reports. Google uses a broad Ads permission; Termwise reads reports and supports narrowly scoped, user-confirmed Pro writes. Secure sign-in briefly exchanges an authorization code through the Termwise service. Access tokens stay in the extension session and are not sent to its AI model.

Reports and rule review stay on your device unless you choose a disclosed cloud analysis. Cloud AI requires separate consent to share the selected query batch, metrics and business context. Read the privacy policy for storage, processing and deletion details.
